About Xpress Point Management Services

Leading the way in HR services and training since 2010.

  1. 2010

    Foundation

    Xpress Point Services Sdn Bhd was incorporated, starting with property management services and outsourced HR solutions.

    Property ManagementHR Outsourcing
  2. 2020

    Growth During MCO

    Surge in demand for HR Services during MCO 1.0 led to expansion and building of our dedicated HR Team.

    Team ExpansionHR Services
  3. 2021

    New Chapter

    Incorporated Xpress Point Management Services Sdn Bhd dedicated to Human Resource Management Services (HRMS) and Training Development Management Services (TDMS).

    HRMS FocusCorporate Solutions
  4. 2020 – 2022

    Legal Excellence

    Successfully helped several corporations win heavily challenged court cases and navigate legal complications, which ultimately helped companies survive the pandemic.

    Legal SupportCourt CasesPandemic Survival
  5. 2023

    Training Expansion

    Began conducting in-house training programmes for clients' employees. Later expanded into public training and officially registered as an HRD Corp Training Provider.

    In-House TrainingPublic TrainingHRD Corp Registered
  6. Present

    Strategic HR Excellence

    Driven by passion for ensuring that with the right employment methods, the right employees can bring positive impact to businesses. Focus on innovation and development through training that aligns with clients' needs across all sectors.

Our founder's journey

iCoL Chua has a personality that empowers optimised employment and HR management. With over 20 years in the industry, iCoL possesses exemplary experience in employee welfare, recruitment, payroll, training, and employment-optimised management.

She maintains strong rapport with local authorities such as KWSP, PERKESO, LHDN, JTK, JKKP and others allowing her to handle HR matters efficiently and in line with client expectations.

As a certified trainer, iCoL brings deep expertise in topics such as Employee Resolution, Conflict Management, Profile Regulation, and other critical HR areas. Through Xpress Point Management Services Training & Development division, she continues to lead impactful, certified programs that align with national standards.
